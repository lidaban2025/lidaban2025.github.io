FormFlow

The response is collected. The decision is not.

Google Forms stores the submission. Someone still has to notice it, email a reviewer, and type Approved or Rejected back onto the row. FormFlow sends that review email and writes the decision to the linked Sheet.

Choice Sync

The list lives in Sheets. The form still shows last month.

Pasting choices by hand drifts. Choice Sync reads the column you select, shows the exact choice diff, and updates the form only after that check. It does not reserve seats or filter choices while someone is filling out the form.

Short answers

FormFlow and Choice Sync

What does FormFlow do?

It is an approval workflow for Google Forms: one reviewer email, an approve or reject link, and the final status on the linked Google Sheet. FormFlow has 1,000+ installs.

What does Choice Sync do?

It updates Google Forms choices from a Google Sheets column after preflight and an exact diff. Choice Sync has 772 installs and a 4.0 rating from 2 reviews.

Where do I install them?

Both install from Google Workspace Marketplace. Use a copied form for the first test, then move the same setup to the live form.
