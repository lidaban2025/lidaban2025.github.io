FORMSUITE CHOICE SYNC: FIRST-RUN CHECKLIST
Updated: 23 October 2026

Goal: a fresh respondent page shows Alpha and Beta from your selected Sheet.
Use a blank or copied Google Form and synthetic values only.

1. INSTALL, THEN RETURN TO YOUR FORM
Install FormSuite Choice Sync from its official Marketplace listing:
https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/formsuite_choice_sync/18856558524
Open a blank or copied Form that you own. Add one dropdown or multiple-choice
question. Open Choice Sync from the Form's Add-ons menu.

2. PREPARE TWO OBVIOUS VALUES
If your sidebar offers Create Alpha/Beta Sheet, use it. Otherwise create a test
Google Sheet and paste these three lines into cell A1:

Test choice
Alpha
Beta

Select that spreadsheet through the add-on. Select its sheet tab and the column
whose header is Test choice (or Choice in the generated sample).

3. LINK ONE QUESTION AND CHECK THE PROPOSED CHANGE
Link the column to your copied Form's choice question. Enable updates if the
setting is shown, then save. Run Preflight and resolve its warnings.
Review the exact before/after choices and approve the preview when requested.
Saving a mapping alone does not update the respondent-facing form.

4. UPDATE AND INSPECT THE ACTUAL RESPONDENT PAGE
Choose Update now. Confirm that the report says at least one question updated.
Open a fresh respondent preview of YOUR copied Form, not our public example.
If Google says your copy is not published, publish it for the intended test
audience, reopen Choice Sync, and open the preview again. Check for both Alpha
and Beta. You do not need to submit a response for this choice-sync check.

5. RECORD THE RESULT, THEN CLOSE THE TEST
[ ] Preflight passed.
[ ] Update now reported an updated question.
[ ] The fresh respondent page actually showed Alpha and Beta.
Only mark these after observing the result. Unpublish the copied test Form
when finished. Do not leave a live test open by accident.

IF A STEP FAILS
Stop at that step. Note only the stage (install, source selection, mapping,
Preflight, update, or respondent preview) and the non-sensitive error text.
Do not send private Form/Sheet URLs, IDs, license keys, or respondent contents.
Help: https://formsuite.dev/formranger/support.html
Full guide:
https://formsuite.dev/formranger/test-google-forms-dynamic-choices-before-launch.html

BOUNDARIES
This is an owner-controlled choice update, not live dependent dropdowns,
inventory locking, booking, payment, or a paid-plan test. No purchase is needed
for this small first check. Ask for support before buying for another use case.
