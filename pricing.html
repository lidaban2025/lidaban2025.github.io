Pricing FAQ

How do I activate my Pro license?
For an eligible paid build, FormSuite emails your Choice Sync key after successful payment. Open Choice Sync in Google Forms while signed in with the purchasing email, paste the key in the sidebar, and confirm that the plan shows Pro. If your installed version has no Pro activation control, do not pay again: contact support with your order reference for activation or refund help. Do not send the full license key.
Is there a free trial?
Neither Free plan is a time-limited trial. FormFlow Free: 50 approval flow runs per month, up to 5 approval levels, unlimited approvers and requesters. Pro: $79/year per admin, unlimited flow runs, up to 5 levels, unlimited approvers. No per-approver fees. Choice Sync Free: 100 syncs per calendar month, up to 3 linked questions. Pro: $29/year, unlimited syncs and linked questions. Install and verify one update or approval in a copied form before considering Pro.
Can I get a refund?
Email support@formsuite.dev within 14 days of purchase or renewal for a full refund. Support handles the refund and confirms how to remove the Pro key from your account. A full refund revokes the affected online entitlement; reopen the add-on to refresh its displayed plan.
