Pricing FAQ

How do I activate my Pro license?
After successful payment, FormSuite emails your Choice Sync key. Open Choice Sync in Google Forms while signed in with the purchasing email, paste the key in the sidebar, and confirm that the plan shows Pro. Contact support if the key or plan status is missing.
Is there a free trial?
Choice Sync includes 100 sync runs per calendar month on Free. Install it and verify one update in a copied form before deciding whether you need Pro. The other add-ons remain free during their public launch.
Can I get a refund?
Email support@formsuite.dev within 14 days of purchase or renewal for a full refund. Support handles the refund and confirms how to remove the Pro key from your account. Refund processing does not automatically revoke an offline activation key.
