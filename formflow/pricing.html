FAQ

What counts as an "approval flow"?

One configured chain of reviewers for a form. Free covers a single Level-1 reviewer chain; Pro removes the limit and adds multi-level chains.

Do approvers need to pay?

No. Only the form owner's account needs a license — reviewers and respondents are always free.

When does Pro launch?

We're onboarding waitlist users first. Waitlist members get founder pricing locked at $29/yr (regular $49/yr).

Is my form data used anywhere?

No. FormFlow stores configuration inside your own form and writes status to your own response Sheet. Nothing is sent to us.
