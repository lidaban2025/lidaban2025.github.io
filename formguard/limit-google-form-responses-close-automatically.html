Set it up in five minutes

  1. Install FormGuard and open it on your form.
  2. Set the total response limit (e.g. 100).
  3. Optional: add per-choice quotas on the questions with limited seats.
  4. Optional: set a close time — and a reopen time if signups run in windows.
  5. Write the custom message late respondents will see.
  6. Test with the form open: submit past the cap and confirm it closes with your message.

Why teachers stop doing this by hand

The classic failure is the overnight overfill: signups close "at 100" in the announcement, but nobody is awake at 2 AM to flip the toggle, and by morning there are 134 responses for 100 seats. The second classic is the popular workshop: one session fills in an hour while others sit half-empty, and the form has no way to say "this option is full, pick another." A cap that only works when someone is watching is not a cap. FormGuard watches so you don't have to.
