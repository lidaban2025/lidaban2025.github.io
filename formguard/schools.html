Why districts choose FormGuard

Privacy by architecture

FormGuard stores its settings inside your own form. We don't collect, transmit, or retain student responses — the signed DPA covers a product that by design has no student data to protect.

Procurement-ready

W-9, signed CA-NDPA (CITE Privacy Services), and district pricing available. California districts can piggyback on the executed San Bernardino City USD agreement (3-year term).

Built for registration day

Response caps, per-option quotas for sessions and seats, scheduled close/reopen windows, and custom closed messages — so signups close themselves, even at 2 AM.

Features

  • Close a form automatically after a response limit is reached
  • Scheduled close and reopen times (reopen at least 1 hour after close)
  • Choice quotas — remove options when a session, slot, or role fills up
  • Custom "form is full" message shown to respondents
  • Owner email alerts on submissions and closures
  • Manual Close/Reopen override from the menu
