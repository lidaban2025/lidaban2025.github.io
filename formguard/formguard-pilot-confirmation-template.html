2. Pilot term

  • Start: date of first deployment
  • End: 12 months from start date
  • Either party may end the pilot earlier with written notice; no termination fee applies.

3. Fees during the pilot

  • License fee: waived for the pilot sites listed in §1 during the pilot term.
  • After the pilot: continued use, additional sites, or district-wide rollout is quoted separately based on actual scope. Current list pricing for reference: School $199/site/yr; District by quote. Pilot participants receive preferred terms on any post-pilot quote.
There are no automatic charges. Nothing is billed without a written quote accepted by the district.

4. What LidaBan Studio provides

  • Deployment walkthrough for staff at each pilot site (up to 3 sessions, 30 min each, online)
  • Email support: response within 1 business day
  • Security overview document and executed DPA copy (attached)
  • Product updates at no extra cost during the pilot

5. What the district provides (minimal)

  • Staff install FormGuard from Google Workspace Marketplace using district Google accounts
  • A named point of contact for scheduling the walkthroughs
  • Optional brief feedback at pilot end (a short call or email summary)

6. Data & privacy

  • FormGuard collects no student data (see attached Security Overview).
  • The executed CA-NDPA covers the pilot in full.
