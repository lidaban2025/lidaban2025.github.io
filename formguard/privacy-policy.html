Privacy Policy

FormGuard | Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. What FormGuard does

FormGuard is a Google Forms™ add-on that helps owners manage response limits, choice quotas, notifications, close messages, and answer availability from inside the form editor.

2. Data we access

FormGuard accesses only the data needed to configure and operate the controls you enable:

  • Google Forms™ data for forms you explicitly configure, including settings, questions, choices, response counts and responses needed to enforce choice quotas. The updated timer build requests the full Forms permission because scheduled tasks have no active editor and must open the configured form by ID. It does not scan unrelated forms.
  • Email address data for optional notifications you configure and for binding a paid license to its buyer's Google account.
  • Purchase and license data when you choose a paid plan, including buyer email, product, transaction identifiers, plan and license expiry. Creem processes checkout and payment information.

3. Data storage

The updated paid build uses an HTTPS license-status check (script.external_request). It sends only the signed license key, active account email and FG product prefix to the FormSuite license service, not form IDs, questions, responses or spreadsheet contents. Confirmed status may be cached for 15 minutes; previously confirmed access has at most 24 hours of outage grace. Initial activation and already-revoked licenses have no outage grace. Updated permissions remain subject to Google review.

Configuration is stored in Google Apps Script™ document, user and script properties. Scheduled jobs store the configured form ID, timing and notification settings in script properties. Form response content is not sent to our licensing service. Purchase and license records are processed separately by Creem and the FormSuite licensing service.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell form responses. Form automation runs inside Google services. Optional notifications send to the address you configure. Paid checkout shares purchase information with Creem; the licensing service uses purchase and buyer-email data to issue an account-bound license and deliver it by email. This does not send form responses to Creem or the licensing service.

5. Data retention

Saved settings may remain in Apps Script storage until removed. Disable scheduled rules and save before uninstalling to remove the associated timer jobs. Uninstalling alone should not be treated as confirmation that every stored setting or trigger has been deleted. Contact support for assistance with workflow or license-record removal; transaction records may need to be retained for payment and accounting requirements.

6. Security

FormGuard relies on Google's infrastructure for authentication, storage, and execution. The add-on does not ask you to move form data into a separate non-Google storage system.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy over time. Material changes will appear on this page with a new last-updated date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact FormSuite support.
