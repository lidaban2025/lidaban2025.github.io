Troubleshooting

Form Mule not sending emails?

Do not immediately reinstall, rerun every row, or switch products. First isolate whether the failure is trigger setup, quota, row data, template conditions, authorization, or an uncertain prior send.

Quick answer

Do not treat a missing status as proof that nothing was sent. Verify the trigger and quota, inspect the exact row and condition, check Gmail Sent plus the recipient inbox, and only then decide whether the row is safe to retry.

Six checks before migration

  1. Trigger: confirm the expected form-submit or timer trigger still exists and belongs to the correct spreadsheet.
  2. Authorization: open the add-on and check whether Google requires the owner to authorize it again.
  3. Gmail quota: verify the sending account still has daily quota.
  4. Recipient: inspect the exact email column and the affected row for blanks, formula errors, or malformed addresses.
  5. Conditions: confirm the row still matches the template's send condition.
  6. Prior outcome: check Gmail Sent and the recipient inbox before retrying a row whose status is unclear.

Match the symptom to the next safe action

Trigger missing or duplicated

Repair the configured trigger, then run Preflight. Do not add multiple replacement triggers blindly.

Prior notification blocked

A sent, sending, or uncertain fingerprint in the 30-day ledger should stay blocked until reviewed.

SEND_UNCERTAIN

Check the recipient inbox, then mark verified sent, reset after the check, or keep the notification blocked.

Watchdog paused

Review the latest errors and uncertain rows before explicitly resuming automatic sending.

FAQ

Why is Form Mule not sending?

Check trigger ownership, reauthorization, quota, the recipient cell, the template condition, and whether the row may already have been accepted for delivery.

Should every failed row be retried?

No. An uncertain outcome requires an inbox check first; a blind retry can create a duplicate notification.

Does FormMerge guarantee exactly-once delivery?

No. It prevents known replays and fails closed on ambiguous outcomes, but it does not claim provider-level exactly-once delivery.
