A narrow, safety-first YAMM alternative

FormMerge is an independent Google Sheets add-on for teams moving a simple YAMM-style operational email: one recipient column, one template, copied-sheet testing, exact output preview, one-time approval, and duplicate-send guards for the rows where a second send would be embarrassing or expensive.

Not a drop-in replacement: YAMM is a mature Gmail mail merge with its own campaign model, tracking, and plan limits. FormMerge does not claim parity with campaign sequencing, open tracking, or marketing automation. It covers one job: reviewed operational email where duplicates are costly.

Copied-sheet migration test

  1. Copy the original spreadsheet; keep the production Sheet untouched.
  2. Replace real recipient addresses with test inboxes you control.
  3. Configure one recipient column and one subject/body template.
  4. Run Preflight, then Preview pending.
  5. Review every exact recipient, subject, body, CC, and BCC.
  6. Resolve exact duplicate outgoing messages.
  7. Approve only the unchanged preview, then send the copied batch.
  8. Confirm each row becomes SENT before enabling any trigger on real data.

Why the guardrails matter for YAMM-style migrations

The most common migration accident is the retried batch: a send hits a daily cap or an ambiguous result, someone reruns it, and half the list gets the email twice. A row marked SENT, SENDING, or SEND_UNCERTAIN is not automatically retried in FormMerge. MailApp does not supply a provider-level idempotency key, so this fail-closed behavior is safer than pretending exactly-once delivery is guaranteed.
