Frequently asked questions

Is this for newsletters?

It is better for operational mail merge: confirmations, updates, notices, and small campaigns.

Can I test before sending?

Yes. Use a copied sheet, inspect the exact pending recipients, subjects, and bodies, remove duplicate payloads, then approve the unchanged preview once.

Where is status tracked?

The sheet records SENT, SENDING, or SEND_UNCERTAIN states. A 30-day audit shows protected replays and manual resolutions; trigger health shows presence, duplicates, last success, last error, repair, and paused state.
