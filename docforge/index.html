Frequently asked questions

Can it create PDFs?

Yes. The workflow supports generating documents and PDF-style outputs from rows.

Do I need a template?

Yes. A Google Docs template gives the generated files their structure.

Will a rerun duplicate successful files?

An unchanged successful row with an accessible output is reused from the manifest. A changed template, header set, mapping, key, naming pattern, folder, or output requires a new one-file proof.
