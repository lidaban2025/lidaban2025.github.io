FormNotifier: one copied form, two inbox checks

Use only a copied form, synthetic Name=Alpha and email inboxes you control.
Do not use student, customer or payment data.

1. Add Name and Email questions. Configure owner alerts and respondent confirmation.
2. Select the Email question if the form does not automatically collect addresses.
3. Operator subject: Test registration: {{Name}}
4. Operator body: A synthetic registration was received from {{Name}}.
5. Respondent subject: We received your registration
6. Respondent body: Hi {{Name}}, we received your registration. This confirms receipt only, not a reserved place or approval.
7. Save. Run Preflight. A sidebar test alone does not prove respondent delivery.
8. Submit one synthetic response on the copied form. Publish only that copy temporarily if required.
9. Verify the separate operator alert and respondent confirmation in the actual inboxes.
10. Disable notifications and unpublish the test copy when finished.

Success means both expected messages arrived with Alpha resolved correctly.
A saved setting or provider acceptance alone is not inbox-delivery evidence.

For support, report only the failed stage and sanitized error text.
Never send private form links, IDs, screenshots, response contents or credentials.
