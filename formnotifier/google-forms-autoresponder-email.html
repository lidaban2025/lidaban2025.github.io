Autoresponder

Google Forms autoresponder email setup

An autoresponder sends an automatic reply after someone submits your form. Google Forms has a basic built-in receipt; here is when it is enough and how to send a custom one.

Quick answer

Google Forms can email respondents a copy of their answers if you enable "Collect email addresses" and set response receipts to "If respondent requests it" or "Always". That is a true autoresponder, but the message is fixed: it is a plain copy of the form answers with no custom subject, no branding, and no next-step instructions. If respondents should receive a tailored confirmation, use an add-on like FormNotifier to send a custom autoresponder template with merge fields.

For many internal forms the native receipt is enough. Use a custom autoresponder when the reply is part of the experience: registrations, applications, support intake, and anything where the respondent expects clear next steps.

Option 1: native response receipt (manual)

  1. Open the form and go to Settings.
  2. Turn on "Collect email addresses" so every respondent has a verified address.
  3. Under "Response receipts", choose "If respondent requests it" or "Always".
  4. Submit a test response and confirm the copy arrives in your inbox.

Limitations to accept: the email content cannot be edited, it only goes to the respondent, and it contains every answer including ones you may not want echoed back.

Recommended setup path

  1. Install FormNotifier from Google Workspace Marketplace and open it from the Extensions menu.
  2. Enable respondent confirmation and choose where the respondent email comes from (collected email or a required email question).
  3. Write a short autoresponder: confirm receipt, restate the key answers, and add the next step.
  4. Run Preflight, save, and use Send test with your own inbox before launch.

Common mistakes

No verified email source

If the form does not collect emails and has no required email question, the autoresponder has nowhere to go.

Untested merge fields

A template that looks fine can break when an answer is empty. Always test with a realistic submission.

Dumping every answer

Long receipts get ignored. Echo only the details the respondent needs to act on.

No failure visibility

Check the recent send report after launch so failed deliveries surface the same day.
