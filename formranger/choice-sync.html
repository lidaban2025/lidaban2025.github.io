Questions, answered

I already tried Form Ranger. Is this the same thing?

Choice Sync was rebuilt from scratch as an independent FormSuite add-on (previously "FormRanger Pro"). It is not affiliated with New Visions Cloudlab's Form Ranger, and it adds the safety check, scheduled sync, and one-click undo.

Will it break my form's section jumps or quiz answers?

No — that's exactly what the safety check is for. Questions using answer-based routing or quiz scoring are flagged and blocked from automatic updates instead of being changed blindly.

What happens if my Sheet column moves or gets renamed?

The next safety check fails loudly instead of syncing garbage — you'll see which file, tab, or header it can't find, fix it, and re-run.

Can my whole school use it?

Yes. Install it on the forms that need it; each mapping is independent. A paid team plan with centralized controls is coming — join the waitlist.
