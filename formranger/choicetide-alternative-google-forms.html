Which workflow fits?

Evaluate Choice Sync for update safety

Run Preflight, inspect the exact added/removed/unchanged diff, review duplicate and look-alike labels with Choice Hygiene, and explicitly approve the output before Update now.

Evaluate ChoiceTide for its own workflow

If Sheet-backed choices with optional capacity are central to your process, check ChoiceTide's current documentation and test a copied form. This comparison does not assert ChoiceTide pricing or claim that it lacks any safety feature.

For rare changes, manually copying a checked list from Sheets into Forms may be enough. A successful editor update does not prove that a live workflow is safe.

Choice Sync safety boundaries

  • Preflight: check source access and mappings; resolve warnings before writing choices.
  • Exact diff before update: review added, removed, and unchanged choices. Choice Hygiene recommendations do not rewrite your source Sheet.
  • Narrow file access: selected files via drive.file rather than broad Drive access. Read the permissions explanation; selected-file access is not a claim of zero permissions.
  • Recovery: Five bounded recovery versions with live-drift checks before restore. Keep a copied form; recovery is not unlimited backup.
  • Not live capacity locking: Choice Sync is not a booking system, payment flow, inventory lock, or respondent-time dependent dropdown.

Test before switching

  1. Keep the current workflow and copy the form.
  2. Select one Sheet column with Alpha and Beta, then map one supported question.
  3. Run Preflight, review the exact diff, approve it, and use Update now.
  4. Open the public respondent preview and verify both values before touching the live form.

FAQ

When should I evaluate Choice Sync?

Evaluate Choice Sync for owner-controlled Sheets-backed choice updates with Preflight, an exact added/removed/unchanged diff, Choice Hygiene, selected-file access, and bounded recovery. It is not a booking or inventory lock.

What does Choice Sync cost?

Free includes 100 syncs/month and up to 3 linked questions. Pro is $29/year, with unlimited syncs and linked questions. Direct Pro checkout is paused; email support@formsuite.dev to confirm upgrade availability before paying.

Is Choice Sync affiliated with ChoiceTide or Form Ranger?

No. Choice Sync is an independent FormSuite add-on and is not affiliated with ChoiceTide or Form Ranger.
